## Course Overview

By the end of this course, you will be able to do the following:
 
- Evaluate limits at points and at infinity graphically, numerically, and algebraically, including using L’Hôpital’s rule.
- Evaluate derivatives using the limit definition and clear, correct, standard notation.
- Differentiate functions involving powers, exponents, sums, products, quotients, and compositions of basic functions. (Basic functions include power functions, exponential functions,
trigonometric and inverse trigonometric functions, and logarithmic functions.)
- Use implicit differentiation to determine slopes of tangent lines.
- Apply derivative techniques to situations involving slopes of tangent lines and instantaneous rates of change
- Use first and second derivatives to explain the behavior of a function, including the shape of its graph.
- Apply derivatives to optimization and related rate problems.
- Translate between Riemann sums and definite integrals.
- Evaluate definite integrals using geometry and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
- Integrate definite and indefinite integrals using substitution, integration by parts, and partial fractions.
- Approximate integrals and identify whether a given approximation technique will over-or under-estimate an integral.
- Explain the connection between limits, derivatives, and integrals.
